# Professional Video Prompts Guide

Generate cinematic, authentic videos that don't look AI-generated.

## Video vs Image Prompts

**Key Difference:** Videos require motion, timing, and camera language

### Images: Static Description
```
"An office with morning light through windows"
```

### Videos: Motion & Cinematography
```
"Camera slowly pans left across office desk as morning sunlight 
streams through windows. Focus on laptop keyboard as fingers type. 
Natural hand movements, realistic speed. Warm light creates soft 
shadows across workspace. 8 seconds, documentary style."
```

---

## The 7 Elements of Great Video Prompts

### 1. **CAMERA MOVEMENT** (Most Important!)
Specify exactly how the camera moves:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Static camera, no movement"
- "Slow push-in toward subject"
- "Smooth pan from left to right"
- "Gentle dolly backwards slowly"
- "Subtle rack focus from foreground to background"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Nice camera movement"
- "Good cinematography"
- "Beautiful shots"

### 2. **SUBJECT MOVEMENT**
Describe what's moving in the frame:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Coffee pours slowly into white ceramic mug, steam rises"
- "Person walks naturally from camera toward window, glances outside"
- "Hands type on keyboard at realistic speed, fingers move naturally"
- "Plants sway gently from breeze through open window"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Movement in scene"
- "Dynamic action"
- "Cinematic motion"

### 3. **DURATION & PACING**
Always specify length:

#### Timing Guidelines
- **4-6 seconds** - Quick cuts, product shots, transitions
- **8-12 seconds** - Standard scene, full action
- **15+ seconds** - Complex scenes, multiple actions

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "10 seconds total. First 3 seconds: pour coffee. Next 4 seconds: take sip. Final 3 seconds: look at window."
- "8 seconds. Slow throughout, no quick cuts."
- "15 seconds with natural pacing, not rushed."

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Fast paced"
- "Dynamic timing"
- "Cinematic pacing"

### 4. **LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE**
Specify light quality and direction:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Golden hour light from camera left, warm shadows across face"
- "Cool morning light through glass, shadows from window frames"
- "Overhead soft lighting, no harsh shadows, neutral color temp"
- "Sunset glow on building exterior, warm tones fading"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Beautiful lighting"
- "Cinematic light"
- "Amazing atmosphere"

### 5. **ENVIRONMENT & SETTING**
Be specific about location:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Modern office, concrete floors, oak desks, matte black steel"
- "Sunlit apartment, vintage wooden furniture, linen curtains"
- "Exterior: brick building facade, green climbing ivy"
- "Coffee shop: wood tables, ceramic cups, warm ambient light"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Nice location"
- "Beautiful environment"
- "Modern setting"

### 6. **CINEMATOGRAPHY STYLE**
Reference actual film/camera techniques:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Shot on 35mm, warm color grading, documentary style"
- "Cinematic color grading, cool tones, professional production"
- "Film photography aesthetic, slight grain, vintage color palette"
- "Documentary, natural lighting, handheld camera feel"
- "Commercial production quality, sharp focus, clean color"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Cinematic"
- "Professional"
- "High quality"

### 7. **REALISTIC DETAILS**
Add authentic imperfections:

#### ✅ Good Examples
- "Real hand movements, natural typing speed"
- "Authentic expressions, not posed"
- "Subtle breathing movements"
- "Natural blink rate if person visible"
- "Realistic liquid physics for pouring"
- "Genuine dust particles in light"

#### ❌ Avoid
- "Perfect movements"
- "Hyper-realistic"
- "Flawless"

## Complete Video Prompt Template

```
[SCENE SETUP]
Starting position, overall environment, key elements visible

[CAMERA MOVEMENT]
How camera moves (static/pan/dolly/zoom/rack focus)
Specific direction and speed (slow, moderate, fast)
Starting point and ending position

[SUBJECT MOVEMENT]
What/who moves in frame
Realistic timing (not rushed, not slow motion unless specified)
Natural gestures and interactions
Object interactions (pouring, typing, etc.)

[DURATION & PACING]
Total seconds
Breakdown if multiple actions
Pacing style (continuous, natural, deliberate)

[LIGHTING & TIME]
Light direction and quality
Time of day or specific lighting condition
Color temperature (warm, cool, neutral)
Shadow characteristics

[SETTING & MATERIALS]
Specific location
Visible materials and textures
Environmental details
Props and their condition (worn, new, etc.)

[CINEMATOGRAPHY STYLE]
Film/photo reference or technique
Color grading direction
Overall visual aesthetic
Production quality level

[AUDIO VISUAL NOTE]
Any specific audio or silent note
Sync points if relevant
```

## Video-Specific Cinema Language

### Camera Techniques
- **Static** - No movement, fixed frame
- **Pan** - Horizontal camera rotation left/right
- **Tilt** - Vertical camera rotation up/down
- **Dolly** - Camera moves toward or away from subject
- **Push-in** - Slow movement toward subject
- **Pull-back** - Slow movement away from subject
- **Rack focus** - Focus shifts between foreground/background
- **Zoom** - Lens focal length change (less realistic)
- **Whip pan** - Fast pan (use sparingly)

### Motion Characteristics
- **Static** - No movement
- **Slow** - Deliberate, contemplative (0.3-0.5x speed)
- **Natural** - Real-world pace (1x)
- **Moderate** - Slightly energetic (1.2x)
- **Dynamic** - Quick, energetic (1.5-2x)
- **Slow motion** - Only specify if needed, changes realism

### Lighting Scenarios
- **Golden hour** - Warm, directional, 1-2 hours after sunrise or before sunset
- **Blue hour** - Cool, twilight conditions
- **Midday** - Harsh, bright, high contrast
- **Overcast** - Soft, diffused, no shadows
- **Practical lights** - Lamps, screens, street lights in scene
- **Backlighting** - Light from behind subject
- **Rim light** - Light along subject edges

### Color Grading Styles
- **Warm** - Increased yellows/oranges
- **Cool** - Increased blues/greens
- **Neutral** - Natural, ungraded appearance
- **Desaturated** - Muted colors, documentary feel
- **Vintage** - Warm, slight fading, film aesthetic
- **Professional** - Clean, balanced, production quality

## Common Video Prompt Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake: No Camera Movement Specified
```
"A person working at a desk"
```
✅ Fix: "Static camera watching person's hands type at natural speed on oak desk"

### ❌ Mistake: Unrealistic Movement
```
"Person moves smoothly across frame"
```
✅ Fix: "Person walks naturally across frame at realistic pace, footsteps visible in shadows"

### ❌ Mistake: No Duration
```
"Coffee being poured"
```
✅ Fix: "Coffee pours for 4 seconds at realistic speed into ceramic mug"

### ❌ Mistake: Too Vague
```
"Beautiful morning scene"
```
✅ Fix: "Morning light 7am, window on camera left. Soft shadows 45 degrees across desk. 
Laptop and coffee visible. Camera static. 10 seconds. Documentary style."

### ❌ Mistake: Impossible Physics
```
"Water floats in air while pouring"
```
✅ Fix: "Water pours naturally following gravity into mug below"

### ❌ Mistake: AI Language
```
"Stunning cinematic masterpiece with hyper-realistic movement"
```
✅ Fix: "Professional production, documentary style, realistic movements, 35mm cinematography"

## Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Specify every camera movement explicitly
✅ Include realistic timing and duration
✅ Name specific materials and textures
✅ Describe lighting direction and time
✅ Reference real cinematography
✅ Use authentic gesture descriptions
✅ Include environmental details
✅ Specify color grading direction

### DON'T:
❌ Use power words ("stunning", "amazing", "beautiful")
❌ Say "cinematic" without describing what that means
❌ Describe impossible physics
❌ Leave camera movement ambiguous
❌ Use AI language ("hyper-realistic", "8K quality")
❌ Rush the timing with "fast-paced"
❌ Over-specify unrealistic motion

## Workflow for Video Generation

1. **Conceptualize** - What's the core action/moment?
2. **Plan Camera** - How will camera move? (Static/pan/dolly/etc)
3. **Describe Motion** - What moves in frame? At what pace?
4. **Set Duration** - How many seconds needed? Break it down.
5. **Light It** - What time of day? What's the light direction?
6. **Style It** - What's the visual reference? What cinematography?
7. **Detail It** - Add authentic materials, textures, natural imperfections
8. **Check It** - Does it avoid AI tropes? Is everything specific?

## Final Tips

1. **Shorter is Better** - 8 seconds is often better than 15 seconds
2. **Camera Movement First** - Decide this before anything else
3. **Natural Timing** - Most AI videos fail on unnatural pacing
4. **Avoid Fast Cuts** - Let scenes breathe
5. **Add Weather** - Real videos show wind, light changes, atmosphere
6. **Include Audio Context** - Even if silent, describe what would be heard
7. **Reference Real Videos** - Watch documentaries, commercials, and study the language

Results: Professional, cinematic videos that don't scream "AI-generated"! 🎬✨
