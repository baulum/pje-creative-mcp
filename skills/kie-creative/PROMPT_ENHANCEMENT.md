# Prompt Enhancement Skill

## Purpose
Enhance image prompts to generate more photorealistic, professional, and authentic images that don't look overly "AI-generated".

## Key Principles

### 1. **Specificity Over Vagueness**
- ❌ "A nice office" 
- ✅ "A modern corporate office at dusk with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the downtown skyline, natural materials like oak and marble, soft ambient lighting from recessed ceiling fixtures"

### 2. **Real-world Context**
- Add specific locations, times of day, weather conditions
- Reference real architecture styles, design movements
- Include realistic details that anchor the image in reality

### 3. **Photography & Film Language**
- Use photography terms: "shot with a 24mm lens", "shallow depth of field", "golden hour lighting"
- Reference film styles: "cinematic lighting", "film photography aesthetic", "Kodachrome color grading"
- Specify technical aspects: "sharp focus on foreground, bokeh background", "high contrast"

### 4. **Avoid AI Tropes**
- ❌ Remove: "stunning", "beautiful", "breathtaking", "ethereal", "magical"
- ❌ Remove: "hyper-realistic", "photorealistic", "8k", "ultra detailed"
- ✅ Add: "candid", "authentic", "natural lighting", "professional photography"

### 5. **Material & Texture Precision**
Instead of: "modern design"
Use: "brushed steel and white oak accents with matte black steel frames"

### 6. **Atmospheric Details**
- Natural: "afternoon sunlight casting long shadows"
- Seasonal: "early autumn, golden light through maple trees"
- Environmental: "subtle dust particles in the light beam"

## Prompt Template Structure

```
[SCENE DESCRIPTION]
[LOCATION & TIME]
[PHOTOGRAPHY/FILM TECHNIQUE]
[LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE]
[MATERIALS & TEXTURES]
[COMPOSITION & FRAMING]
[MOOD & STYLE]
```

## Example Transformations

### Before (Generic AI Look)
"A beautiful modern office with computers and plants, stunning lighting"

### After (Authentic Professional)
"A corporate workspace captured during mid-morning, natural light streaming through large windows with subtle glare on monitors. White oak desks with cable management, pothos plants in ceramic planters on shelves. Shot with 35mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field focuses on keyboard in foreground. Soft shadows across concrete floors. Professional photography, natural color palette with warm undertones."

## Implementation Rules

### For Interior/Architecture:
1. Specify materials explicitly (not just "modern")
2. Add time-of-day for lighting direction
3. Include real textures and finishes
4. Reference actual architectural styles or designers

### For Product/Lifestyle:
1. Show real usage context
2. Add natural imperfections (worn edges, reflections)
3. Specify photography equipment/techniques
4. Include environmental interaction

### For People/Portraits:
1. Describe natural expressions and poses
2. Add specific lighting setup (ring light, three-point, etc.)
3. Reference portrait photography styles
4. Include candid/authentic direction

### For Nature/Landscape:
1. Specify exact time and season
2. Add weather conditions
3. Reference landscape photography schools (Adams, Weston, etc.)
4. Include foreground/middle ground/background depth

## Negative Prompts Strategy

Add to counter AI artifacts:
- "not AI generated, not digital art"
- "no oversaturation, natural color grading"
- "no plastic appearance, authentic materials"
- "no perfect symmetry, natural composition"
- "subtle imperfections, real-world wear"

## Quality Checklist

✅ Specific materials and colors mentioned?
✅ Time/season/weather included?
✅ Photography technique specified?
✅ Lighting direction clear?
✅ Removed generic descriptors?
✅ Authentic details included?
✅ Professional terminology used?
✅ Avoided AI tropes?
